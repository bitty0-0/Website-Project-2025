About the Show

Mr. Robot follows Elliot Alderson, a cybersecurity engineer and hacker who suffers from social anxiety and dissociative identity disorder. He is recruited by an underground hacking group called "fsociety" to take down the corrupt conglomerate E Corp.

Main Cast

  • Rami Malek as Elliot Alderson
  • Carly Chaikin as Darlene Alderson
  • Christian Slater as Mr. Robot
  • Portia Doubleday as Angela Moss
  • Martin Wallström as Tyrell Wellick

Why Watch Mr. Robot?

Mr. Robot is one of the most accurate portrayals of cybersecurity and hacking in television history. The show dives into real-life hacking techniques, ethical dilemmas, and the psychological aspects of cyber warfare.
